### 0.2 Permanence is a missing QUANTITY, not a missing re-queue — the fan-out corrected me here

My own first reading was that accept-once-and-never-re-score is a permanence mechanism. **It is
not, and §1 is right.** `s` is a pure function of the facet's three vertex coordinates plus
`acceptTol`; live triangles never have their corners rewritten (only two coordinate-write sites
exist, `:1705` and `:2428`, both default OFF). Re-scoring an accepted facet returns the *identical
double*. A re-queue buys exactly nothing. Likewise the `563,600 dropped = 20.1% of split attempts`
figure at `:3151` is **not** a permanence mechanism: against **774** terminal `unresolved`, 99.86%
of strandings are transient, and S27-to-quiescence independently caps all re-examination at ~4.6%.
**Corridor walling and budget truncation are both refuted as significant causes.** Record this: the
two mechanisms that matter at scale are the footprint cone (#1) and the blind predicate (#2).

### 0.3 THE TWO LINES THE FAN-OUT MISSED — and they change §6

The synthesis's own §7 states the gap correctly — *"The angle-mode calibration constant (the
analogue of `tolMm = bar/8`) does not exist"* — and then §6 proposes to sweep the **chord** knobs.
Those do not sit together: §7.0 of the state doc says chord/position is *not* what binds. §6 as
written answers *"is generation a better frame?"* and leaves *"can the generator target the bar that
actually binds?"* untouched.

**The angle mode is two lines, in production code, with both principal curvatures already in hand.**
`src/renderers/webgpu/parametric/conforming/tierC/surfaceMetricField.ts:15-19` declares **exactly two
modes, UNIFORM and CHORD** (`h₃D = √(8·tol/κ_max)`) — code-verified. And `anisoCurvatureMetric`
already solves the full shape operator:

```ts
// surfaceMetricField.ts:192-195  — the CHORD law, in metric form
const k1 = qq !== 0 ? qq / qa : -qb / (2 * qa);
const k2 = qq !== 0 ? qc / qq : -qb / (2 * qa);
const mu1 = clamp(Math.abs(k1) / (8 * tol));   // unit metric edge ⇔ h₁ = √(8·tol/|k₁|)
const mu2 = clamp(Math.abs(k2) / (8 * tol));
```

Turn across an edge of length `h` along principal direction *i* is `Δθ ≈ |k_i|·h`, so the ANGLE
analogue is `h_i = θ*/|k_i|`, i.e. `μ_i = (k_i/θ*)²` — a substitution on those two lines. The
isotropic counterpart is one line beside `kappaMaxAt` (`:90`), which already returns
`max |principal curvature|`.

***And this is the one construction the campaign's two hardest ceilings both fail to bound.***
§0i.3's `18.33×` at 5%-over-area is the fractional-knapsack optimum of **any isotropic field** — an
anisotropic angle metric is not that object. §0i.4's reason the `1.89×` anisotropy prize is
unreachable is that **subdivision** cannot align to parent long axes (p50 28.43° misaligned) — a
generator does not subdivide. Both ceilings are escaped by the same two lines.

⚠ Honest caveat: this does **not** make the 1° bar cheap. My own independent arithmetic (κ = 1 mm⁻¹:
`h_chord = √(8·0.01/κ) = 0.283 mm` vs `h_angle = 0.01745/κ = 0.0175 mm`, 16.2× in edge ⇒ ~263× in
area) lands on the same order as the record's ~155× full-clearance figure, derived a different way.
That is corroboration that the price is real, not an artefact — the anisotropic claim is that the
*isotropic knapsack bound* on that price does not apply, and it is untested.

⇒ **AMENDMENT TO §6: run the sweep, but add an angle-mode arm.** Marginal cost is two lines plus one
night, and one night then answers both the frame question and the binding question instead of one.

### P0 — Instrument the four things generation currently hides *(byte-identical)*

Four counters, all in `_strataConformBisectL.test.ts`:

- **(a) The floor return.** `consider` (:2807) returns before scoring when the longest edge is under `FLOOR_MM` (1.5 µm), and the only counter is inside `if (tighten !== null && …)` — `tighten` is null on every default path, so **this exit increments nothing.** Make it unconditional and record the facet in `unresolved` with kind `'floor'`. A counter that reads 0 everywhere and cannot be made to fire is a dead instrument and must be removed, not shipped.

**Does NOT fix:** anything. It measures. That is the point — the campaign has a documented case (K=8) of a lever passing 12/12 one-sided bars while destroying the perpendicular ruler, and four of the kill lines below are unreadable without these counters.

### P2 — The two under-splits nobody has looked at

- **`metricLen2` (`inhouseMetricMesh.ts:269`) samples M at the edge midpoint only**, bilinearly interpolated off the sizing grid. It is not a max, not an integral. An edge whose midpoint lands in a coarse cell while its interior straddles a fine one is never split **at any `splitThresh`**. Fix: evaluate at t ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75} (or endpoints + midpoint) and take the max. ~6 lines.
- **`buildInhouseMetricMesh` forces `resU: sizeRes, resT: sizeRes` (:255).** On the S94 cone (H=120, r̄≈45) the u-axis spans 2π·45 = 282.7 mm against t's 120 mm, so a grid cell is 1.778 mm circumferentially × 0.755 mm vertically — a **2.36:1 chart anisotropy**, worst in exactly the direction Gothic's relief varies. Fix: `resU : resT ∝ 2πr̄ : H`. ~5 lines. Field build cost rises 2.36× — ≈230k → ≈540k rA evals, negligible against 858M.

Note the residual is **linear** in the curvature under-estimate: κ_alias = κ_true/f gives h over-sized by √f and realised sag = tol·f. Not sub-linear.

**Kill line.** Ceiling: over-bar chord area at equal triangle count must fall ≥1.10× versus the P1 base arm. Floors: point count must not rise more than 1.5× *(the whole point is that it should rise — the ceiling is on the cost, the floor is that the area must actually move; if points rise and area does not, the split predicate is not the binding term)*; minAngle p05 must not fall below 0.90×. **Non-vacuity:** the 3-point max must change the split count by > 1% — if it changes nothing, midpoint sampling was adequate and the finding is dead.

### P3 — Per-quad diagonal chosen against the locus *(default path, all 17 styles)*

`_strataConformBisectL.test.ts:1266-1267` emits both triangles of every quad across the same diagonal:
```
addT(grid[j][i], grid[j][i1], grid[j + 1][i1]);
addT(grid[j][i], grid[j + 1][i1], grid[j + 1][i]);
```
So the seed's edge-direction field is three fixed parametric directions over the entire wall, independent of feature orientation. Change: probe **both** diagonals with `locateKink`; take the one with no crossing; tie-break on the shorter 3-D chord. ~20 lines, no new points, no change in triangle count at seed.

Honest bounds. The diagonal is one of three edge families, so this addresses at most ~1/3 of edges, and the ceiling is unmeasured until P0(b) ships. It is not "the aligned seed, cheap": full locus alignment delivers 9,363 vs 10,641 by count (12%) but **4.7× by rate** (uniform grid ≈12.6% of ≈84,200 edges vs aligned 2.676% of 349,848). Expect a fraction of the rate win, not of the count number. Its real claim is coverage: it runs on the **default** path and on the ~16 styles for which `ALIGNED_SEED` throws outright (`:1181-1189`, any detected C0 z-step).

**Kill line.**
- **Ceiling:** seed crossing **rate** (P0(b)) must fall ≥1.15× on Gothic **and** on at least one z-stepped style (CelticTriquetra).
- **Floors, all required:** (i) seed `gridOverCap` must remain 0 on styles where it is 0 today, and `gridWorstAR` must not rise above 1.05× — a diagonal choice changes facet shape and can manufacture the exact class the guard exists to stop; (ii) at convergence with acceptTol fixed at 3.5 µm, triangle count must not rise more than 2%; (iii) the S100 gate's back-facing **area** on the surface scope must not worsen beyond 1.05×; (iv) H1 witnessed over-bar area must not rise above 1.05×.
- **Non-vacuity:** the number of quads where the two diagonals disagree must be > 5% of quads. If the choice is almost always forced, the lever is inert and should be deleted rather than shipped as a no-op with a cost.

**Does NOT fix:** the fixed-point population (88.4% of crossing edges have one endpoint already on a constraint). Those are unreachable by any diagonal choice; they need the metric route or nothing.

- **The stranded set, and the risk of enlarging it.** Every added birth-time refusal converts a refinable facet into a permanent one. P3 and P4 are shape-neutral or shape-improving by construction; **P7 is not**, which is why it carries a triangle-count ceiling and a hard P5 precondition.
- **Treads, caps and floor fans.** The S100 gate scores only the outer wall (facets within 0.010 mm of `rA`) and sets aside 14,473 facets / 39.077 mm² on CelticTriquetra as NOT-SCORED, because their orientation needs the surface *they* belong to and the gate does not have it. For a closed pot, tread orientation is **unmeasured, not passing.** Nothing here changes that; it needs a tread-wall analytic surface handed to the gate.
- **Multi-valued geometry.** Every vertex is `R(θ,z)` — verified: exactly two coordinate-write sites in the whole driver (`:1705`, `:2428`), both default-off. Overhangs still need a separate emitter.
- **Position.** The cone bounds orientation. Position already closes at ~1.04× and the cavity arm shows a fixed-vertex-set position win exists. None of this targets position; the floors exist to stop it regressing.
- **The 88% floor itself.** Shrinking footprints converges (0.69× per level after its null, against a 0.85× kill), but it is bought with elements. The entire claim of this proposal is that *generation* buys the same footprint for ~1.93× fewer elements than bisection — not that it buys it free. If P1's cost floor trips at 3×, that claim is wrong for Gothic and should be reported as such.

# DRIVER AWARENESS — a change set for STRATA-001

## 0. The diagnosis in one sentence

The driver's **demand** and its **operator** are mismatched in opposite directions: the demand is one winding-invariant scalar that cannot see the defect class the eye sees (`consider()` at `_strataConformBisectL.test.ts:2801-2837` is `sagAdaptive(t) <= localAcceptTol(t)` and nothing else), while the operator is a single-edge bisection whose only response to "I cannot do that legally" is `return false` → `unresolved.set(t, kTop)` (`:3154`) → ship. Every previous attempt failed by fixing exactly one side:

| Arm | Which side it touched | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| S20 `ADMIT_NORMAL` accept veto | demand only | **0 of 5,313,594 accepts refused**; H2 21.379 → 55.652 µm; refused |
| S100 sweep `triangleNeed` conformance-first | demand only | crease-crossed AREA **3.42× worse**, defect relocated **181×** into `move-deferred` |
| S27 `STRAND_RETRY` to quiescence | re-examination only | 25 of 4,588 resolved, worst **UNMOVED**, 0.07% of budget |
| S99 `MOVE43H` as a real arm | operator only, wrong objective | crossings **rose 1.21×** against a <1,600 kill line |

**The design rule this yields, and the one every proposal below obeys:**

> A demand may only be raised where an operator can discharge it **in the same pass**, and where the driver can **detect at demand time, for free, whether that operator is available**. If it is not available, the facet must be **accepted and NAMED**, never re-queued into an invisible dead end.

That last clause is the S100 antidote. S100's failure was not that the demand was wrong — its gate-A collapse (97.27% → 9.17% "accepted by the blind ruler") proves the diagnosis was right. It failed because an undischargeable demand had nowhere to go but `move-deferred`, which is unmeasured. `unresolved` is the same trap on the heap side.

## P0 — Instrument the refusal and the placement. Byte-identical.

**Why first:** three of the four biggest open questions in this codebase are unanswerable today because 773,079–85,775 aspect refusals collapse into one integer and ~768,000 *successful* splits leave no record of what rung placed them. Nothing else can be priced until this exists.

**Files and functions:**

| File / function | Change |

## P1 — Give the operator a legal move: protector cascade on the size path

**The gap.** `shapeAdmits` records *exactly which neighbour blocked the split* — `lastShapeOffenderT = t` (`:1504`, `:1514`, `:1526`) — and `cascadeConform` (`:1882-1940`) already implements the Rivara discharge of that obligation: push the offender's longest edge as a protector obligation, recurse, depth-capped at `S9_DEPTH`, metered against `S9_BUDGET`, every child through `bisectAt` so S1/S2/S20 still apply. It is reachable from four sites — SNAP (`:1741`), CONFORM_FIRST (`:2885`), the fossil pass (`:3941`), and `deshardConform` (`:4088`/`:4178`) — **and none of them is the ordinary size path.** When `refineDirected` picks an edge for SIZE and every rung is refused, the driver learns the cage face and throws it away.

**Change.** `_strataConformBisectL.test.ts` → `splitEdge`, after the nudge ladder at `:1825`, before `return false`:

```
if (SIZE_CASCADE && lastBisectShape === 'ar' && lastShapeOffenderT >= 0) {
  szFired += 1;
  const oc = cascadeConform(a, b, -1);   // t0 < 0 ⇒ isCrossing false ⇒ midpoint obligations only
  szSplits += cascadeStat.splits; /* …outcome counters… */
  if (oc === 'conformed') return true;
}
return false;
```

`t0 = -1` is load-bearing: `isCrossing = tv >= 0` (`:1901`) is then false, so the crossing-retreat branch is disabled and this is a pure protector cascade. No new algorithm, no new placement rule. Gate: `const SIZE_CASCADE = envOn('PF_CB_SIZE_CASCADE') && !SWEEP && !GPU_RANK;` — default OFF, so the control stays byte-reproducible.

**Why this is not S8's refuted result.** S8-PROD2 measured `SELF-BLOCK 18,102 of 18,102` — but that was the **post-loop** fossil population, which is *censored at the cap by construction*: the main loop already refined every pair fat enough to split legally, so post-loop survivors sit at the AR boundary. The in-loop claim is the opposite and is the driver's own (`:1734-1735`): *"Trains are still SHALLOW at first encounter (the S8 pilot conformed 94.8% in exactly this regime)."* That has never been measured on the size path. P0's `both-inadmissible` counter tells you the answer before you spend a night on it.

**Why this is not on the refuted list.** It is not "bigger connectivity search" (no flips, no cavity DP, no collapse); it is not an AR-cap fidelity claim (the cap is unchanged at 50 and is not being sold as fidelity); it is not `MOVE43H` (no vertex moves).

**Kill line — pre-registered, both directions:**

| Quantity | Instrument | Kill |
|---|---|---|
| `unresolved` count | driver | must fall **≥ 25%** (control 774 Gothic / 560 Voronoi). Fails if it rises at all. |
| gross allocations | driver | must stay **≤ 1.15×** control. |
| honest over-bar **AREA** | `certifyTriangle`, whole-mesh, tol 0.010 mm, `witnessedComplete` respected | must not worsen by more than **1.02×** |
| **FLOOR (anti-vacuity)** — back-facing AREA | `s100BackFacingGate`, k=8 covering, wall-scoped | must not rise above **1.10×** control. A cascade that clears `unresolved` by manufacturing slivers passes the first three bars and fails here. |

## P2 — A two-tier honest accept, with a splittability guard

**What the accept predicate must measure instead of infinite-plane sag.** Three quantities, in cost order. The critical property of the first two is that they are **pure functions of one facet against the analytic surface** — no neighbour is read — so the pop loop's `bsReuse` shortcut (`:3146`) keeps its licence and no dirty-stamp machinery is needed. That kills the "we must first rebuild the re-queue" objection.

| Tier | Quantity | Where | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Screen** | orientation chord `2·sin(θ/2)·diam` vs the analytic normal at the centroid (`normAngOf`/`orientOf`, `landFlipPass.ts:258-277`), plus the sign test `footBackT` (`:1486`) | CPU, in `consider()` | **~5 rA amortised** (`admBestDot` is 5 rA; `footBack` short-circuits at the centroid on ~all facets) | flag only |
| **Certificate** | `certifyTriangle` (`_facetTruthLib.ts:400`) with an explicit `sampleCap`, `witnessedComplete` honoured | CPU, only on screen-flagged facets | **~2k–20k rA/facet (wide guess)** | verdict |
| *(rejected)* | k=8 covering orientation | — | — | *end-of-run gate only* |

**The S92 discipline, stated as a rule:** the centroid normal is a **screen, never a verdict**. S92 re-scored the flip's banked 3.73× at 1.050×/0.984× precisely because a centroid key was scoring itself. A conservative one-sided screen is legitimate; reporting its number as the result is not. The verdict is `s100BackFacingGate`'s k=8 covering, read on the shipped STL.

**Change 1 — `consider()` (`_strataConformBisectL.test.ts:2801`), a fourth veto in the identical shape to the S20/S29/CERTACCEPT lines, with one clause none of them has:**

```
if (s <= at && HONEST_ACCEPT) {
  const oChord = orientChordT(t);                  // screen, ~5 rA
  if (oChord > ORIENT_BAR) {
    if (splittableNow(t)) { haForcedPush += 1; hpush(t, s); return; }   // ← the S100 antidote
    haDeclaredResidual += 1;
    residual.set(t, oChord);                       // ACCEPTED AND NAMED
  }
}
```

`splittableNow(t)` = `shapeAdmitsBest(e)` (`:1534`) on any of the three edges. That is **12 `aspect3` evaluations and zero rA** — pure arithmetic on coordinates already in cache. This is why the antidote is affordable: the driver can ask "can I discharge this?" for free, and it never raises a demand it cannot meet.

**Change 2 — the certificate tier.** For facets the screen flags **and** that are splittable, escalate to `certifyTriangle` before spending a split, so the driver does not refine on a false positive. Reuse the existing `certifiedAccept` scaffolding at `:2750-2799` (which already does the escalation loop and the `witnessed + cov/n <= tol` test) but gate its entry on the screen instead of on every accept. **That single change is the difference between S36CA's NO-GO and an affordable arm:** S36CA measured 858 M → 6,061 M rA (7.1×) because CERTACCEPT ran on *every* accept; screen-gated to ~1% of accepts it is ~1/100th of that increment.

**Change 3 — the report.** `residual` is printed as a named class (count, AREA, orientation-chord p50/max) and, per the missed finding in the dossier, the export soup at `:4349` gains a **refusal to ship silently**: if `residual.size + unresolved.size > 0` the run tags the STL and the report prints the honest area. Today the driver holds a list of its own known-bad facets at export time and does not consult it.

**Kill line — pre-registered, both directions:**

| Quantity | Instrument | Kill |
|---|---|---|
| back-facing AREA (wall-scoped) | `s100BackFacingGate`, k=8 covering | must fall **≥ 2×** vs control. |
| honest position over-bar AREA | `certifyTriangle` whole-mesh, tol 0.010 | must not worsen by **> 1.02×**. *(S20 died here: 0.00128% → 0.00200%.)* |
| gross allocations | driver | **≤ 1.25×** control |
| rA evals | driver | **≤ 1.30×** control. *(S36CA's 7.1× is the reference NO-GO.)* |
| **FLOOR — non-vacuity** | driver | `haForcedPush > 10,000`. **If the veto fires zero times the arm is VOID, not a PASS** — that is exactly S20's failure mode, and it is why P3 is a hard prerequisite. |
| **FLOOR — no silent relocation** | driver | `haDeclaredResidual` must be **printed with its AREA**. An arm where declared-residual AREA exceeds the back-facing AREA it removed is a relocation, not a fix — that is S100's 181% `move-deferred` signature caught at design time. |
| **FLOOR — heap drains** | driver | `heap left == 0` and not `timeCapped`. A non-draining arm is a **trajectory, not a verdict** (the driver's own words at `:2699`). |

**Cost.** ~120 lines + ~80 lines of test. Screen: derivation says accepts ≈ live facets ≈ 1.14 M × 5 rA ≈ 6 M on a 858 M budget = **+0.7%**; S20 *measured* the same wiring at **+0.3%** (909 M vs 906 M), so this is the right order and I trust the measured number over mine. Certificate: **wide guess, +5–15% rA**, entirely dependent on the screen's flag rate, which nobody has measured — the pilot must report the flag rate before the full arm is budgeted. Triangles: **guess +5–20%**, because an honest accept demands more splits.

**Does NOT fix:** crease conformance (deliberately — see §2 below); position error on facets whose *footprint* is already too large for the local turning (§0f.1's 88.33% Gothic / 93.97% Voronoi irreducible share is a hard ceiling on every fixed-footprint remedy, and this proposal keeps footprints); anything on a mesh whose seed was born over the cap; treads and caps, which `s100BackFacingGate` explicitly does not score (`s100BackFacingGate.ts:373-375`) — 14,473 facets / 39.077 mm² on CelticTriquetra are **UNMEASURED, not passing**.

## P3 — The `FLOOR_MM` silent exit (prerequisite for P2)

**The bug.** `consider()` line `:2807`:

```
if (le < FLOOR_MM) { if (tighten !== null && tighten.scaleForSphere(...) > 1) tightenFloorHits += 1; return; }
```

`FLOOR_MM` = 1.5 µm. `tighten` is `null` unless `PF_CB_TIGHTEN` names a loci file. **On the default path this increments nothing and records nothing.** A sub-floor facet is neither scored, nor queued, nor entered in `unresolved` — it is a third, invisible exit alongside accept and shape-refusal. The same test recurs in the `bsReuse` guard at `:3146`, so a survivor that refines down to sub-floor edges mid-drain vanishes from the heap while the run still prints `heap: 0 left, worst-left 0.000 µm`.

This is not speculative: S20's own post-mortem names it as **the** reason the accept-side orientation test fired zero times across 5.3 M checks — *"Sub-floor facets are therefore never admission-tested at all, and a sub-floor facet is exactly the kind that ends up pointing the wrong way."*

**Change.** `_strataConformBisectL.test.ts` → `consider` (`:2807`): count unconditionally (`nFloorAccepts += 1`, plus an AREA accumulator), record `unresolved.set(t, kTop=s_unmeasured)` under a new `unresolvedKind` value `'floor'`, and print it. Mirror the counter at the `bsReuse` guard (`:3146`) so the two floor exits are distinguishable.

**Kill line:**
- **CEILING:** STL md5 **byte-identical** to control. This changes no geometry; if it does, it is wrong.
- **FLOOR (anti-vacuity):** `nFloorAccepts` must be **> 0** on at least one shipped style. If it is zero everywhere, the class does not exist and P2's zero-fire risk has a different cause — which is itself the finding.
- **FLOOR:** the reported `floor AREA` must be reconcilable with the honest over-bar AREA — if sub-floor facets are >5% of the failing area, `FLOOR_MM` is a fidelity constant masquerading as a performance guard and must be re-derived.

**Cost.** ~10 lines. Zero rA, zero triangles, zero runtime.

**Does NOT fix:** anything by itself. It makes the h⁰ verdict — "this facet is as small as the driver will ever make it and it is still wrong" — visible for the first time since R1, which is the one class the campaign has repeatedly failed to separate from artefact refusals.

## P4 — Re-examination: vertex-star dirty re-offer

**The honest framing.** Re-scoring an *accepted* facet is a provable no-op: every CPU rank mode is a pure function of the facet's three vertices plus `acceptTol`, and live triangles never have their corners rewritten (`:3129-3141`, which is the driver's own argument for `bsReuse`). **But `shapeAdmits` is NOT pure-local** — it scores children against `edgeMap`'s incident list, which changes as neighbours refine. So a shape-*refused* facet genuinely can become splittable later, and that is the only population where re-examination has any theoretical content.

**Change.** `_strataConformBisectL.test.ts` → `bisectAt` (`:1563-1576`), after the split loop succeeds: for each of the new vertex `m`'s star members, if any is in `unresolved`, `consider()` it and delete it from the map. Bounded, O(deg) per split, no passes, no outer loop.

**Why not more iteration.** S27 built the maximal version — re-offer every strand to quiescence against the fully-refined mesh — and got 3 passes of 8 (**stopped because a pass resolved zero**), `unresolved` 4,584 → 4,563 (0.5%), **worst 95.473 → 95.473 µm, UNMOVED**, on 0.07% of a 200,000 budget. The existing one-shot resume (`:4225-4237`, flag-gated, and it **did** run on the shipped arm — the report reads `resume 499 splits on +1996 of 20000`) resolved 187. So the ceiling on all re-examination, standalone, is measured at **~4.6% of the stranded set**.

**I therefore rank this low, and it is conditional on P1.** With a protector cascade in the size path, a neighbourhood change becomes *actionable* rather than merely observed, so the ceiling may move. Run it only as P1's second arm.

**Kill line:**
- **CEILING:** allocations ≤ 1.03× the P1 arm; wall ≤ 1.05×.
- **FLOOR:** `unresolved` must fall by **≥ 10%** *beyond* what P1 delivered alone. Below that, it reproduces S27 and is declared NO-EFFECT.
- **FLOOR (anti-vacuity):** `nDirtyReoffered > 0` **and** `nDirtyResolved / nDirtyReoffered > 0.02`. S27's own decay curve (24 → 1 → 0) is the null it must beat.

**Cost.** ~15 lines. **Guess** <1% triangles, <1% runtime.

**Does NOT fix:** the accept predicate (re-offering an accepted facet is arithmetically inert); anything on the 97.6% ordinary-facet majority; the AR-cap corner itself.

## P5 — `PF_CB_MID3D_MAXSHIFT`: an unreviewed assumption, falsified on disk

**No code change required — this is a sweep of an existing env var.** `placeAt` (`:1376-1381`) clamps the 3-D chord solve at `MID3D_MAXSHIFT = 0.25`, and the source comment at `:239-242` justifies the clamp with *"the MEASURED bias is ~0.09 … so 0.25 never binds on the observed population; … a run where this fires is a finding, not a tuning knob."*

**That condition is met and nobody read the report.** `research/tools/potscope/gothicarches_ring_DS-HT_S40VFC.report.txt:19` — `3-D midpoint: 1440179 solves, mean |s-frac| 0.06105, max 0.56172, clamped 64146` = **4.454% of the driver's own split population clamped, with a max wanted shift of 0.56 — 2.25× the cap.** Other arms read 1.66%, 5.27%, 7.5%.

Note what the clamp is and is not: because `chordParam` solves a function monotone from `−frac·|AB|` to `+(1−frac)·|AB|`, the clamped return `frac ± 0.25` lies strictly *between* the raw parametric point and the chord-correct point — it is a **partial** correction, never a restoration of the raw bias. And crease placements bypass `placeAt` entirely (SNAP at `:1730`, REPROJECT at `:1784`, `cascadeConform` at `:1903`), so the clamp cannot pull a vertex off a locus.

**Arm.** `PF_CB_MID3D_MAXSHIFT` ∈ {0.25 (control), 0.40, 0.50}, one flag, on the byte-reproducible control config.

**Kill line:**
- **CEILING:** honest over-bar AREA must not worsen; allocations ≤ 1.05×.
- **FLOOR:** `nMid3dClamped` must **fall monotonically** with the raised cap (it is already counted and printed at `:5275`). If it does not, the counter is measuring something other than the clamp.
- **FLOOR (anti-vacuity):** `nShapeRefusedAR` must be reported alongside. A raised cap that reduces clamping while raising aspect refusals has traded a placement defect for a strand, and that is a loss even if the position ruler improves.

**Cost.** Zero engineering. One run per arm.

**Does NOT fix:** anything structural. It closes a documented, falsified design assumption and removes it from the list of unpriced confounds in every future A/B.

## 2. The three-step landing sequence

**Step 1 — measure, change nothing (1 day, byte-identical).**
P0 + P3 + P5's read. Assert STL md5 identity as the gate. Output: the committed-rung histogram, the LONGFALL three-way split, the guard-drop count, the floor-accept count and area. **Decision point:** if LONGFALL's `both-inadmissible` bucket is small, P1's ceiling is small and P1 should be descoped to a pilot; if `nFloorAccepts` is zero, P2's zero-fire risk has a different cause and P2 must be re-derived before it is built.

**Step 2 — operator before demand (1 night per arm).**
P1 alone, one flag, against the byte-reproducible flag-off control (never against a committed STL — those are not reproducible). Then P4 as P1's second arm only if P1 passed. **Decision point:** P2 is not started until an arm exists in which a demanded split is legal. Building P2 first reproduces S20 and S100 exactly.

**Step 3 — demand, screen-gated and splittability-guarded (2–3 days build, 1 night per arm).**
P2 pilot first: run the screen alone, report the flag rate, and budget the certificate tier from it. Then the full arm. Verdict on `s100BackFacingGate` k=8 covering + whole-mesh `certifyTriangle`, never on the driver's own key and never on the centroid screen's own number.

P6 runs in parallel throughout — it touches no driver code and shares no artefact with steps 1–3.

## 3. What this change set does NOT fix — globally

1. **The footprint cone.** §0f.1's per-footprint normal-cone bound (88.33% Gothic / 93.97% Voronoi / 99.16% LowPoly of over-bar AREA irreducible for its own footprint, sound two-sided bracket, cross-checked at max |Δ| = 0.000e+0 rad) is untouched, because every proposal here preserves footprints. **This bounds the whole set at roughly the 5.90–11.03% re-orientable share for orientation.** Only shrinking footprints — i.e. more splits, which P1 and P2 do buy — moves it, and the convergence rate is 0.69×/level *after* its own coplanar-split null (0.428×), not the raw 0.208×.
2. **Sizing and anisotropy.** These are generation levers. Free placement certifies the same geometry at 1.615 leaves/parent against LEPP's 3.120 — a 1.93× structural prize no splitter can recover, because bisection's factor-2 granularity is already the finest a subdivision operator has and its node set is confined to existing edges. The vehicle is the `M = g/h²` remesh; note that the anisotropic kernel and its metric-in-circle flip already exist in `src/.../tierC/` and that H4 measured the *isotropic research fork* with both named knobs at their bad settings.
3. **Facets born over the cap in the seed** (3 of 254,926 Gothic at worst AR 85.13, 2 surviving to the STL). Outside every operator's reach; the fix is a seed-side acceptance assertion in `_strataAlignedSeed`.
4. **Treads, caps and floor fans.** `s100BackFacingGate` scores only the outer wall; 14,473 facets / 39.077 mm² on CelticTriquetra are UNMEASURED. For a closed pot this is a real gap and needs the tread's own analytic surface.
5. **Gothic's shipped strand population has never been honestly scored.** The 774 facets' recorded severity is `unresolvedKind: 'plane-pop-key'` — the driver naming its own banned ruler. S96's 84.29% PROVEN-FAIL / 253.307 µm figures are **Voronoi's**, not Gothic's.
6. **The `keyInversions` confound.** 37,867–175,924 inversions are consistent with both "child born worse" and "the capped-at-64 lattice finally resolves the smaller child at a finer absolute pitch." P0 does not disambiguate them; a per-split parent/child key assertion would, and is worth building if step 1's data points that way.
