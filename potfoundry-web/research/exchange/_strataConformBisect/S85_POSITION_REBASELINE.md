# S85 — THE POSITION RE-BASELINE (authoritative)

**Every "position over-bar" number this campaign published was measured with `sagAdaptiveRaw`, an
INFINITE-PLANE distance. This file replaces them with a two-sided `certifyTriangle` reading at the
10 µm PRODUCT bar.** Generated by `research/tools/s85Assemble.cjs` from the per-mesh summary JSONs.

- Instrument: `certifyTriangle` (`research/bridge/_facetTruthLib.ts`), `tol = 0.010 mm`, `nMax = 512`.
- Three buckets, **never folded**: PROVEN-FAIL (`witnessed > tol` — a real point at a real distance),
  PROVEN-PASS (`bound <= tol` over the whole triangle, gaps included), UNKNOWN (level/sample ceiling).
- Tool + pre-registration: `research/tools/s85PosRebase.ts` (header written before the first run).
- Runner `research/tools/run-s85-pos-rebase.sh`; queue `research/tools/s85-queue.sh`; per-facet
  ndjson checkpoints in `s85rebase/` (a killed run resumes and re-scores nothing).

## THE THREE QUESTIONS — answered from the tables above, computed not transcribed

### Q1 — Does the AR-cap sweep's ordering survive?

| cap | driver HEADLINE µm | driver over-bar | honest PROVEN-FAIL rate ±1σ | honest fail AREA frac ±1σ | uniform in-sample honest max µm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AR50 (S39CTL) | 47.282 | 75 | 0.2860% ±8.4% | 0.03534% ±19.6% | 240.059 |
| AR55 | 32.317 | 63 | 0.2180% ±9.6% | 0.03329% ±19.8% | 240.059 |
| AR65 | 23.563 | 32 | 0.2020% ±10.0% | 0.03216% ±19.0% | 240.059 |
| AR90 | 10.83 | 1 | 0.1340% ±12.2% | 0.04009% ±28.4% | 460.513 |

- **COUNT:** monotone across 50→55→65→90? **YES**. AR90/AR50 = **0.469×** (pre-registered kill line ≤ 0.50). 1σ intervals disjoint? **YES** (AR50 ≥ 0.2621%, AR90 ≤ 0.1504%).
- **AREA:** monotone? **NO**. AR90/AR50 = **1.134×** — the AR-90 mesh has the **LARGEST** proven-fail area of the four.
- **MAX (unbiased sample):** AR90/AR50 = **1.918×**.
- The blind ruler claimed **4.37×** improvement.

**Pre-registered H-R1** ("the ordering does NOT survive") is **REFUTED on its own kill line** — the kill line was written on COUNT alone and COUNT does order the arms the way the blind ruler did. But the improvement is **2.13× not 4.37×**, and by AREA (1.13×) and by MAX (1.92×) the lever is neutral-to-worse. **A count-only table would have said "4.4× win"; the honest reading is "2× fewer failing facets, each of them bigger and the worst one twice as bad".**

**And no arm is anywhere near the bar.** The mesh the driver reports as "10.830 µm, 1 facet over 10 µm" has **1527 PROVEN-FAILING facets** (scaled from a 4.39% sample) and a **PROVEN 460.513 µm** exceedance — 46× the product bar.

### Q2 — Is the under-report ratio stable enough to correct old numbers?

Mesh-level ratio (honest scaled fail count / driver over-bar count) ranges **21.1× to 1526.7×** across 9 meshes — a spread of **72.4×** against a pre-registered kill line of 1.5×. Meshes where the driver reports **0** over bar have an **undefined (infinite)** ratio and are excluded from that spread, which understates it.

**H-R2 CONFIRMED: no single correction factor exists.** Table 3 shows why at the facet level — the per-facet `witnessed/plane` ratio has p50 ≈ 1.00 (the plane ruler is *right* for a typical facet), ~36% of facets where it OVER-reads, and a max of 200–1500×. The error is not a scale factor, it is a TAIL. Published position numbers can be **discarded, not corrected**.

## What is NOT measured (stated up front)

- **The honest whole-mesh MAX.** Unreachable at this budget (`certifyTriangle` runs ~1–28 facets/s
  here; a 1.14 M-facet mesh is ~11–260 core-hours). Table 2's max is a lower bound over a targeted
  union; Table 1's max is an in-sample max at the stated coverage. Neither is "the mesh max".
- **H2** (surface → mesh). `certifyTriangle` is H1: points ON the facet, measured to the surface. A
  missing-material defect the mesh does not cover is invisible to it.
- **Orientation.** Position only.
- **Meshes not run** are simply absent from the tables above rather than estimated.
