## The Unifying Pattern

Every standing wall is the same object: a **C0 (or near-C0) discontinuity in the surface field** — a crease, step,
cliff, cusp, or hash-boundary where the radius/height derivative is undefined or effectively infinite over a
sub-cell width — colliding with a mesher and a metric that both assume a locally smooth, single-valued field. The
walls-lens named this directly (weave = occlusion/`max()` steps; risers = near-vertical cliffs; Gothic =
infinitely-thin C0 cusps; Voronoi = hash breaks; the sliver/straddle floors = the near-C0 step no finite mesh can
chord). The wins-lens is the exact photographic negative: every durable win was a **placement/classification fix
validated by a surface-perpendicular (true-3D) measurement, never a density or budget fix** — put vertices/edges
*exactly where the geometry demands* (metric kernel `M=g/h²`, locked constraint edges, `guardManifoldAlways`) and
*decide where a lever applies by measuring the real perpendicular defect*. The two lenses reconcile into one
statement: **the discontinuity locus is the shared structure of the failures, and landing geometry on that locus —
while measuring perpendicular to it — is the shared structure of the successes.** The dividing line the whole ledger
converged on is the smoothness of the field *at the feature*: smooth/thin-ridge discontinuities (Gyroid, Gothic) are
conformable and density-responsive; step/occlusion/cliff/hash discontinuities are exclude/accept/irreducible —
because a piecewise-linear mesh and a same-(u,t) chord can only faithfully represent a field that is C0-continuous
with bounded derivative across the feature.

## The Frontier Thesis

**Stop building an extrinsic, smooth-field mesh and then patching discontinuities into it after the fact; instead
make the discontinuity graph the *primitive* the mesh is grown from — build the 1D feature/seam skeleton as a
protected complex FIRST and refine the smooth patches between its branches — so the entire "conform vs exclude vs
recover" wall class dissolves into an ordinary boundary-of-domain problem rather than an intrusion the mesher must
survive.**

## Reconciliation with the SOTA scout and the leverage lens

- **Bet 1 = SOTA "discontinuity-first / feature-skeleton-first."** Promoted to #1 because it is the only approach
  that attacks the *thesis-level* assumption (build order / constraint model) rather than one wall, and its
  discriminator **reuses loci we already have** (near-zero setup cost). It subsumes the recovery-ceiling wall, the
  crest wall, and the u-seam special-case in one construction.
- **Bet 2 = the leverage lens's single bet, verbatim.** Kept as its own #2 (not merged into #1) because it is
  *independently* the cheapest measure-only probe and it dominates the *smooth-sharp* class even if the skeleton
  rebuild is deferred. Its own kill-criterion is the honest fork: if analytic-metric-alone doesn't reproduce the
  conform win, leverage collapses into Bet 1 (the win was edges/topology, and Stage-B productionization becomes the
  play). Complementary, not redundant — run both discriminators; they cross-validate.
- **Bet 3 = SOTA "cross-field / Instant Meshes."** Kept because it is the *only* candidate that targets the
  transition-fan **sliver** class directly (a quality wall Bets 1–2 don't touch), and its discriminator is a
  one-afternoon offline binary run — extraordinary cost/info ratio.

**Dropped / demoted, with reason:**
- **SOTA "Signpost intrinsic (geodesic) Delaunay"** — *demoted, near-duplicate of Bet 1 at higher cost.* Solves the
  same crossing-loci and near-C0-crease walls (intrinsic edges cross legally, lie on the surface) but requires the
  signpost data structure + an intrinsic Lawson stack, whereas protected-PLC reuses our loci + off-the-shelf CGAL.
  Keep its unique claim (legal intrinsic crossings) as the **fallback** if Bet 1's PLC refiner stalls at apex crossings.
- **SOTA "Locally-uniform anisotropic (II,I) Riemannian metric"** — *demoted, folds into Bet 2.* A strictly stronger
  version of Bet 2 (full anisotropic vs curvature-floor), but the walls-lens flagged aniso as **over-stretching
  smooth controls** (SuperellipseMorph %<20° 10.4→27.2) → selective-only. Run it *only if* Bet 2's isotropic
  curvature-floor confirms but plateaus on across-crease chord; then the (II,I) generalized-eigen correction is the
  next increment. Not first-line.
- **3D-direct surface remesh** — *dropped, pre-registered REFUTED* (E-2026-06-26): CVT ties gmsh fidelity, QEM is a
  sliver factory, both hit the same near-C0 straddle floor; the relief gap is a sizing/budget limit, not a
  UV-vs-3D-topology limit. No payoff, more cost.
- **"Just add density" / budget** — *dropped, doubly refuted.* Crest under-shoot is density-invariant (Gothic
  1.51@0.8M → 1.39@2M; BasketWeave *worsens* at 4.5M) and ours is 110–213× denser than SOTA yet more slivered.
- **No-lock / inject-only rescue for weave/braid** — *dropped, REFUTED* (G≈B to 3 decimals). The weave/braid trio is
  step/occlusion-discontinuous (multi-valued height field) and stays EXCLUDE; none of the three bets claims to
  conform it. Its genuine fix is a separate future bet (seam-cut / correct height source + the CPU↔WGSL post-warp
  locus divergence), explicitly *not* conflated here.

## The One Bet To Run First

**Bet 1 — discontinuity-first feature-skeleton meshing — starting with the GothicArches arch-apex crossing patch,
because the loci already exist (`featureGraph`, ~48k lines), the discriminator is a skeleton-only dev build on a
single style, and a pass simultaneously refutes the recovery-ceiling wall AND validates the paradigm shift for the
price of one experiment.**

_Honest caveat:_ it fails if the smooth **patch-interior** refinement between skeleton branches reintroduces the
same transition-fan slivers or under-sizes the relief that Bet 2 targets — i.e. protecting the discontinuity graph
fixes *recovery and crest placement* but leaves the *interior quality/sizing* wall untouched, in which case Bet 1
must ship together with Bet 2/Bet 3 rather than alone. (Also: CGAL/PRS SOTA basis is **(verify)** — not
tool-confirmed against the current papers.)
