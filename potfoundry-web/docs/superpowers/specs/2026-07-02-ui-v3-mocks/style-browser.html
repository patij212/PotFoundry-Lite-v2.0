The style browser — how do you meet 15+ styles?

Styles are PotFoundry's soul: SuperformulaBlossom, GothicArches, DragonScales, Voronoi… Each has its own parameters. How should users discover, compare, and apply them? All options use real GPU-rendered thumbnails of your current pot, not stock images.

A — Gallery grid in the panel (recommended)

2-column grid of live thumbnails inside the Style tab; click applies to the stage; the chosen style's parameters unfold directly beneath. Everything in one place, zero mode switches, scales to 30+ styles with scroll.

Blossom · parameters

B — Filmstrip under the stage

Styles as a horizontal strip beneath the pot; arrow keys or clicks step through and apply live; the panel holds only parameters. Very browsable and stage-centric — but the strip eats viewport height and competes with the onboarding hint line.

Choose a style

C — Showroom overlay

"Browse styles" opens a full-screen gallery with large previews and category filters; picking one returns you to the stage. Most theatrical, best for 30+ styles someday — but it's a mode switch, and comparing a style against your tuned pot means bouncing in and out.
