Why this scales to 100+

  • The panel never grows. Current style + favorites/recents (max ~8 thumbs) + parameters. Whether you have 15 styles or 500, the daily surface stays calm.
  • The showroom absorbs all scale: category chips (needs a category field added to the style registry), search-as-you-type, lazy GPU thumbnail rendering with an IndexedDB cache so 100 previews don't re-render every open.
  • Semi-transparent overlay: your pot stays faintly visible behind it; hovering a style live-applies to the real pot — comparison without leaving the library.
  • Favorites (♥) bridge the two surfaces — shopping in the showroom stocks the panel's shelf.
