Section 1 — Design language: “Studio at Dusk”

Matured from v2's DNA (Fraunces + Inter + gold survive) but recomposed: warmer neutrals replace v2's blue-greys, one gold does less work, and every text style has exactly one job. Rendered below in the real fonts.

Rule: gold appears only on things you can do — the CTA, the active tab, slider thumbs, focus rings. Never on decoration. Status colors are desaturated to stay inside the craft mood.

Motion & depth

  • Keep v2's easing family (they're good): enter cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), move "clay on wheel" (.22,.61,.36,1). Drop the overshoot spring from large surfaces (it made the mobile sheet lurch).
  • Durations: 120ms controls · 200ms panels · 320ms showroom/modals. prefers-reduced-motion collapses all to instant.
  • Two shadow levels only: float (panel, toolbar) and overlay (showroom, dialogs). Glass = blur(12px) + 88% tint, used for panel/toolbar/chips only.
