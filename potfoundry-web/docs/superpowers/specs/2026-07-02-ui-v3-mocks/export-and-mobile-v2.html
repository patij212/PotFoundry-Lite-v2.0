The five mobile rules
  1. Tabs live at the sheet's bottom — segmented bar + Export CTA sit in the natural thumb arc, not at the sheet's top. Top of phone holds only brand + account (rare taps).
  2. Every parameter is a fat instrument — 52px rows: drag anywhere on the row to scrub (fill shows position), ± steppers for single ticks, tap the value for a keypad, haptic detents at defaults/limits. No 3px slider thumbs.
  3. Camera cooperates with the sheet — each sheet stop reframes the pot into the visible stage area (peek = full pot, half = framed above sheet, full = rim peeking). No more clipped pots.
  4. Stage controls float bottom-left — camera reset + undo as two thumb-height buttons on the stage; two-finger twist orbits; double-tap recenters. The 15-icon toolbar is gone entirely on mobile.
  5. Landscape = side panel — the sheet becomes a right-hand panel (40%), reusing the desktop panel composition; showroom opens full-screen with press-and-hold live preview on your pot.
