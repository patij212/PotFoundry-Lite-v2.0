The signature — pick where the boldness goes

One memorable element; everything else stays quiet. Both candidates come from the pottery world itself — one is informational, one is theatrical. We ship one as the soul of the app (the other can follow later, subordinated).

Height 120 mm

and dragging the curve moves the sliders

A — The Living Blueprint (recommended)

A live technical cross-section of your pot drawn at the top of the Shape tab — gold generatrix curve, wall thickness, real dimension ticks, like a vessel drawing on a craftsman's bench. It updates as you drag sliders, and you can grab the curve itself to shape the pot directly. Informational boldness: it explains the parametric math visually, differentiates every screenshot of the app, and no competitor has it.

flick to spin — clay-on-wheel inertia
on load, the pot rises from a clay disc (700ms, once)

B — The Potter's Wheel

The stage behaves like a wheel: a subtle turntable ring under the pot, flick-to-spin with clay-like inertia, and a once-per-session entrance where the pot rises from a flat disc through its own profile. Theatrical boldness: unforgettable first impression and demo material — but it's felt only in motion (screenshots don't carry it) and needs careful restraint to not feel gimmicky by hour three.

Whichever you pick leads; the other can arrive later as a quiet supporting act (e.g., Blueprint leads, wheel-inertia flick ships as a viewport nicety without the ring & ceremony).
