The eight improvements

1

Grouped pill toolbar

History · View · Scene as three separate pills with hover labels — replaces the current strip of ~15 identical icons that overlaps the gizmo on mobile.

2

Panel with a voice

Serif italic section headings ("Size", "Profile"), essentials first, values in gold mono aligned right. Labels never truncate — the panel is one coherent instrument, not stacked widgets.

3

Inline advanced disclosure

Each section ends in a hairline "advanced" seam that names what's inside (wall, drain, bell, spin). Expands in place, remembers its state. Newcomers never meet the deep end uninvited.

4

Real preset thumbnails

"Start from" shelf with actual GPU-rendered pot previews — the thumbnail render pipeline already exists in the codebase (universal WGSL shader). Kills the "Preview unavailable" placeholders.

5

The scene is part of the design

Warm studio backdrop with a soft pedestal shadow replaces the navy blueprint grid that currently fights the gold UI. The pot always looks like it's being exhibited, not debugged.

6

Sticky export footer

Quality tier, live triangle/file estimate, and one gold CTA always visible at the panel's foot — export is the product's promise, it never scrolls away or gets cut off.

7

Humane status, tucked gizmo

"24,412 triangles · 0.20 mm detail · WebGPU" in quiet mono instead of cryptic △0 ⬡0 glyphs. Axis gizmo lives bottom-right, out of the toolbar's territory.

8

Whispered onboarding

A single italic hint line on the stage replaces the white welcome card. First-run glow on the preset shelf. Zen mode (Z) fades everything to just the pot.
