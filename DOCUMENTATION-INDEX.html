Original project evidence

Source What remains authoritative What has been superseded
DASA+Sea+Ice+-+Phase+B+(2025)+System+Design+_+Architecture.doc Existing modified F3 uses Molex SL: pin 1 GND; pin 2 record/S358; pin 3 power/S403; pin 4 status. Button signals float near 3.0 V. Status levels are approximately 0.2 V OFF, 0 V idle and 1.7 V recording/turn-on. Its Feather table labels status on D13; v0.4.0 correctly uses ADC pin A3. Short-ON/long-OFF is now Alexis's 1 s / 2 s.
dasa sea ice block diagram - Phase B v2.4.pdf Shore 5V is detected before the hold-up power bank; the bank keeps the Feather/F3 powered through shore loss. TAS5634/TAS1020/USB-audio amplifier labelling is historical; v0.4.0 outputs generic 3-wire I2S.

Known discrepancies and their current resolution

Topic Historical statement Current resolution
Status pin D13 A3 analog input. D13 unused.
Shore input Not assigned / old D5 SD-CS D5 through measured 22k/33k divider.
F3 connector Implied in block diagram Exact Molex mapping recovered from architecture: 1 GND, 2 record/S358, 3 power/S403, 4 status.
Power timing Short ON, long OFF Alexis spec: power-ON 1 s, power-OFF 2 s (bench-tune on the F3).
Amplifier interface TAS5634/TAS1020/USB audio Generic I2S: D1 BCLK, D10 LRCLK, D11 DATA. Selected amplifier compatibility remains a bench item.
Step 4 Deferred Implemented in v0.4.0; physical acceptance remains outstanding.
